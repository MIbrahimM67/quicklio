# Quicklio Off-Page SEO & Community Playbook

## Goal
Earn qualified users, genuine mentions, feedback, and editorial links. Do not optimize for raw backlink count.

## Channel rules

### Reddit
- Read each subreddit’s current rules before posting.
- Solve the problem first; do not lead with a link.
- Disclose ownership when mentioning Quicklio.
- Do not use fake third-party language such as “I found this tool.”
- Do not create multiple accounts to seed recommendations.
- Respect promo days/threads and moderator removals.
- Use UTM parameters only where links are allowed.

Current channel notes (2026-09-29):
- `r/SideProject`: appropriate for transparent maker/product feedback posts.
- `r/website`: use the moderator-created self-promotion thread.
- `r/word`: useful for problem-first participation around PDF-to-Word workflows; avoid sales language.
- `r/webdev`: project promotion is commonly limited to Showoff Saturday.
- `r/InternetIsBeautiful`: not a priority; 2026 removals cite a “No Business Tools” rule.

### Product Hunt
Use for meaningful Quicklio product milestones, not every small feature.
- Join with a personal maker account.
- Build community presence before launch.
- Do not pay hunters or buy traffic/upvotes.
- Launch only when the product is live, clear, polished, and immediately usable.

### Outreach
Good outreach target:
- article/tutorial already discussing the exact problem;
- resource page where Quicklio adds a genuinely useful missing workflow;
- educator/creator whose audience repeatedly encounters the problem;
- niche publisher or community with strong topical relevance.

Bad outreach target:
- generic high-DR list with no topical match;
- automated “link exchange” requests;
- paid dofollow placement;
- mass guest-post network.

## Community post formula
1. Start from the user problem or what was learned while building.
2. Explain one useful insight even if the reader never clicks.
3. State what Quicklio does differently.
4. Be explicit about limitations.
5. Disclose that we built it.
6. Ask for specific feedback, not upvotes.

## PDF to Word launch angles
- “I built a browser-local PDF-to-Word converter because sensitive documents should not need an upload.”
- “PDF-to-Word converters often create files that look right but become unusable when edited; I built diagnostics around that problem.”
- “Scanned PDF vs selectable-text PDF should be detected before conversion, not after a bad result.”

Avoid claiming perfect formatting. The strongest differentiator is privacy + honest diagnostics + editable-text-first output.

## Measurement
For each allowed link, use source-specific campaign tracking and review:
- sessions;
- `tool_started`;
- `tool_completed`;
- `download_clicked`;
- reviews/feedback;
- earned mentions/links;
- repeat/return visits.

A channel that produces clicks but no completed tasks is low quality.
