Frequently asked questions

What photos work best?

Subjects with a clear contrast against the background are easiest. Busy backgrounds may need background removal or color cleanup first.

Can I make a white silhouette instead?

The exported SVG/PNG is black by default, but you can recolor it in most design tools after export.

Is SVG really vector output?

Yes. Quicklio traces the binary silhouette boundary into SVG path data rather than embedding the source photo.
