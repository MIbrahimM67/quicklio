Make a silhouette from a photo

Quicklio converts a high-contrast image into a solid filled silhouette with a transparent background. It works best when the subject is clearly separated from the background by brightness or contrast.

Control the edge instead of accepting an automatic guess

Adjust threshold, contrast, speck removal, smoothing, and inversion until the shape matches the subject you want. Export a transparent PNG for graphics or SVG for vector-based craft workflows.

Note: this is threshold-based silhouette extraction, not AI semantic background removal. Busy photographs with similar foreground and background tones may need a dedicated AI background remover.
