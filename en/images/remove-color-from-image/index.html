Remove a color from an image online

This color remover from image tool is designed for a different job than automatic background removal. You choose the exact color yourself, then Quicklio makes matching pixels transparent across the whole image. It works well when a graphic, screenshot, icon, signature, scan, or illustration contains a flat color that you want to remove everywhere.

How to remove one color from a picture

  1. Upload a PNG, JPG/JPEG, or WebP image.
  2. Click the color you want to remove in the original preview, or choose it with the color picker.
  3. Start with a low Tolerance value and increase it until nearby shades are included.
  4. Use Edge softness if anti-aliased pixels leave a visible outline.
  5. Preview the result on transparency, white, dark, or a custom color, then download the transparent PNG.

What tolerance does

Real images rarely contain only one exact RGB value. JPEG compression, anti-aliasing, scanning, lighting, and resampling can create nearby shades around the color you selected. Tolerance controls how far Quicklio can move away from that selected RGB color and still treat a pixel as a match.

Remove white, black, green, blue, or any custom color

  • Remove white paper from scanned line art, handwriting, or signatures.
  • Remove black, green, blue, or another flat color from simple graphics.
  • Make a solid green-screen or blue-screen color transparent when the subject does not contain the same shade.
  • Delete one brand or accent color from an icon or illustration before recoloring it.
  • Remove a solid screenshot or UI color while keeping the rest of the image intact.
Important: this is a color-based transparency tool, not AI subject segmentation. If a selected color also appears inside the subject, those pixels can be removed too. Reduce Tolerance or use the Logo Background Remover when you only want to remove an edge-connected solid background.

Why the result downloads as PNG

PNG supports transparent pixels. JPG does not, so exporting to JPG would replace transparency with a solid background.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove a specific color from an image?

Upload the image, click the color you want to remove, then adjust Tolerance until that color and the nearby shades you want gone become transparent. Keep Tolerance low when the same color also appears in details you want to preserve.

How do I make one color transparent?

Select that color from the image or with the color picker, run the removal, then download the result as PNG. PNG keeps the transparent pixels in the exported file.

Can I remove white from a picture?

Yes. Pick white from the image and adjust Tolerance. If you only want to remove white that touches the outer background while keeping enclosed white details, use Quicklio’s Logo Background Remover instead.

Can I remove black, green, blue, or another custom color?

Yes. The picker accepts any RGB color, and you can click directly on the original preview to sample it.

Will this remove the selected color everywhere?

Yes. Matching pixels are evaluated across the whole image. This is useful for color-key removal, but it also means matching pixels inside the subject can become transparent.

Is my image uploaded?

No. The image is decoded, processed, previewed, and exported by your browser on your device.
