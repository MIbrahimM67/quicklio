Use in PDF Editor

Remove white background from a signature

Instead of treating every light pixel as pure white, the tool estimates the paper color from image edges so slightly warm or gray scans can be cleaned more naturally.

Protect thin signature strokes

Tolerance and edge softness are separate controls. Raise tolerance carefully, then use softness to smooth the transition around anti-aliased ink.

Prepare a signature for documents

Trim transparent whitespace and export PNG for documents, presentations, forms, or Quicklio’s browser PDF editor.

Frequently asked questions

Why did part of my signature disappear?

The tolerance is probably too high. Reduce it until thin strokes return, then use a little edge softness instead.

Can I make a blue signature black?

Yes. Turn on recoloring and choose the ink color you want.

Does Quicklio store my signature?

No. Processing runs in your browser. Your image is not uploaded by this tool.
