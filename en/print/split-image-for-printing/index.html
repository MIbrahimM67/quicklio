How to print a large image on multiple pages

Upload one image, choose A4 or Letter paper, then select a page grid or an exact finished size. Quicklio splits the image into a multi-page PDF so you can print the large picture on standard sheets and assemble them afterward.

Split an image for printing on A4 or Letter paper

Use this image splitter for printing when a picture is larger than a single sheet. Choose how many pages wide and high you want, or enter the final poster dimensions and let Quicklio calculate the page coverage.

How to print a poster on multiple pages

Create a tiled poster PDF, print every sheet, trim where needed, and line up the neighboring pages. Overlap and cut guides give you extra room to tape or mount the poster cleanly.

Choose a grid or exact poster size

A simple page grid is useful when you already know how many sheets you want. Exact-size mode is better when the final poster must reach a specific width and height in inches or centimeters.

Print at Actual Size / 100%

For predictable assembly, print the generated PDF at Actual Size or 100%. Avoid Fit to Page, Shrink, or Scale to Fit because those settings can change the dimensions and make adjacent sheets harder to align.

Use overlap and cut guides for assembly

A small overlap gives you room to trim and join pages without leaving gaps. Keep the cut guides enabled when you want visible alignment marks while assembling a large image or poster from multiple printed pages.

Can I split one image across A4 pages?

Yes. Choose A4 paper, set the number of pages wide and high or enter the finished poster size, then create the tiled PDF. The image is divided across the required A4 pages.

Can I print a picture on multiple Letter pages?

Yes. Select Letter as the paper size and Quicklio will create a multi-page Letter PDF from the single image. Print the pages at 100% and assemble them in the planned grid.

How much overlap should I use?

A few millimeters is usually enough for trimming and taping. The default 5 mm overlap works well for many home poster projects, but you can reduce or increase it depending on how you plan to join the sheets.
