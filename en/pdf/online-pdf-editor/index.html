Open a PDF to start.
This version adds and arranges content on PDF pages. It does not directly rewrite arbitrary existing PDF text/font objects. Edited or rotated pages are flattened on export; untouched pages remain original PDF pages.

Annotation-first editing

Use movable text boxes, images/signatures, highlights, shapes, and freehand marks for the common edits people make to forms and documents.

Page organization built in

The page strip doubles as an organizer: drag to reorder, select a page, rotate it, or remove it before export.

What v1 does not pretend to do

Directly editing arbitrary embedded PDF text while perfectly preserving its original font/layout is a different and substantially harder capability. Quicklio clearly separates that from annotation editing.
