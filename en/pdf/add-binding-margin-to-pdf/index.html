Binding margin vs gutter

A binding margin is extra space near the bound edge so text and graphics do not disappear into a spine, punch holes, or binding hardware. On duplex documents the inner edge alternates, which is why mirrored margins matter.

Keep page size or increase it?

Keep page size when output still must be A4, Letter, or the original paper size. Increase page width when your workflow accepts a wider sheet and you want to preserve content scale.

How much margin should I use?

Use the requirement supplied by your printer, bindery, university, publisher, or binding method. There is no universal correct value for every production process.
