Bleed vs trim

Trim size is the final cut size. Bleed extends artwork beyond that edge so small cutting shifts do not expose an unwanted white line.

Crop marks

Crop marks indicate where the finished piece should be cut. The marks are placed outside the bleed area.

Review before production

This tool simplifies common bleed preparation, but it does not replace a full commercial preflight workflow for complex files.
