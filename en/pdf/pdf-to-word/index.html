    Which mode should I use?
    • Hybrid: recommended for reports, assignments, manuals, illustrated documents, diagrams, and most normal PDFs.
    • Editable Text: best when you mainly care about rewriting the words and do not need complex graphics preserved.
    • Preserve Layout + Images: best when the page must look as close as possible to the PDF and editability is less important.
    • Scanned pages can use optional English OCR in Hybrid or Editable Text mode.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Convert PDF to Word without uploading
    This converter parses or renders the PDF inside your browser and builds the DOCX on your device. Your document is not sent to a Quicklio conversion server. That matters for contracts, resumes, school work, business documents, and other files you may not want to hand to an unknown upload service.
    For another local PDF workflow, use the Online PDF Editor when you only need to annotate, sign, draw, or reorganize the PDF rather than convert it.
    
    
    Hybrid PDF to Word: editable text plus diagrams
    Hybrid is designed for the exact middle ground most people want. Quicklio keeps selectable PDF text as real editable Word text, but renders the non-text PDF content separately. That visual layer can include diagrams, charts, images, vector artwork, rules, boxes, backgrounds, and other graphics.
    The important detail is that Quicklio suppresses the PDF's text-painting operations when it builds the visual layer. That avoids baking a duplicate copy of the selectable text into the screenshot behind the Word text.
    
    
    Preserve PDF formatting, images, and diagrams
    PDF and Word store documents differently. PDF fixes text and graphics at exact coordinates, while Word normally reflows paragraphs and objects. Quicklio provides three conversion strategies instead of pretending one method is perfect for every file.
    Preserve Layout + Images renders each selected PDF page at high quality and places that complete page into the DOCX at the matching page size. This keeps embedded images, diagrams, charts, vector graphics, columns, backgrounds, and visual spacing together. The tradeoff is that the complete page behaves like a snapshot rather than separately editable Word objects.
    
    
    Convert scanned PDF to Word with OCR
    If you can select the words in your PDF, Quicklio can usually extract that text directly. A scanned PDF may contain only page images, so there is no editable text to recover until OCR is used. Quicklio detects scan-like pages and, when you enable OCR in Hybrid or Editable Text mode, renders those pages locally and runs on-device English OCR to create editable Word text.
    OCR accuracy depends on scan quality, language, handwriting, and page layout, so review important text after conversion. Preserve Layout mode can keep the original scan exactly as it appears even when you do not need OCR.
    
    
    Make PDF text editable in Word
    Hybrid and Editable Text modes build a real DOCX with editable text rather than flattening every page. The converter keeps the original PDF page size and retains more heading size, bold/italic emphasis, paragraph alignment, relative indentation, paragraph spacing, and page boundaries when the PDF exposes enough positioning information.
    • Editable text instead of page screenshots.
    • Paragraph grouping and likely headings.
    • Basic bold/italic signals.
    • Left, center, and right alignment where it can be inferred.
    • Relative indentation and page dimensions.
    Complex tables, overlapping objects, forms, uncommon fonts, and exact multi-column geometry can still need cleanup because the PDF does not contain Word's original document structure.
    
    
    Use page ranges for large PDFs
    You do not have to convert the whole file. Enter a page range such as 1-8 or 2,5,10-14 to create a smaller Word document from only the pages you need. Visual rendering automatically reduces resolution for very long selections to control browser memory and DOCX size.
    If file size is the real problem instead, try Compress PDF.
    
    
    PDF to Word questions
    Is this PDF to Word converter free?
    Yes. You can convert supported PDFs to DOCX without creating an account, entering an email address, or paying Quicklio for the conversion.
    Can I keep diagrams and still edit the text?
    Yes. Choose Hybrid. Quicklio keeps selectable text as editable Word text and preserves detected non-text visual content separately, including diagrams, charts, images, vector graphics, rules, boxes, and backgrounds.
    Does PDF to Word keep images and diagrams?
    Yes. Hybrid preserves non-text visuals while keeping text editable. Choose Preserve Layout + Images when the closest possible visual match matters more than editing individual text and graphics.
    Can I convert a scanned PDF to editable Word?
    Yes, for many scans. Choose Hybrid or Editable Text and enable OCR scanned pages before analysis. Quicklio runs English OCR in the browser and adds the recovered text to the DOCX.
    Can I edit the Word file after conversion?
    Yes in Hybrid and Editable Text modes. Preserve Layout mode gives a closer visual match, but its complete page snapshots are not individually editable.
    Does Quicklio upload my PDF?
    No conversion upload is required. Parsing, scan detection, OCR when enabled, visual rendering, and DOCX creation happen in your browser.
