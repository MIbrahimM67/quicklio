    Which mode should I use?
    • Editable Text: reports, essays, letters, resumes, and PDFs whose words need editing.
    • Preserve Layout + Images: brochures, diagrams, charts, forms, multi-column documents, illustrated reports, and visually complex pages.
    • Scanned pages can use optional English OCR in Editable Text mode.
    
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    Convert PDF to Word without uploading
    This converter parses or renders the PDF inside your browser and builds the DOCX on your device. Your document is not sent to a Quicklio conversion server. That matters for contracts, resumes, school work, business documents, and other files you may not want to hand to an unknown upload service.
    For another local PDF workflow, use the Online PDF Editor when you only need to annotate, sign, draw, or reorganize the PDF rather than convert it.
    
    
    Preserve PDF formatting, images, and diagrams
    PDF and Word store documents differently. PDF fixes text and graphics at exact coordinates, while Word normally reflows paragraphs and objects. Quicklio now gives you two conversion strategies instead of pretending one method is perfect for every file.
    Preserve Layout + Images renders each selected PDF page at high quality and places that page into the DOCX at the matching page size. This keeps embedded images, diagrams, charts, vector graphics, columns, backgrounds, and visual spacing together. The tradeoff is that the visual page behaves like a page snapshot rather than separately editable Word objects.
    
    
    Convert scanned PDF to Word with OCR
    If you can select the words in your PDF, Quicklio can usually extract that text directly. A scanned PDF may contain only page images, so there is no editable text to recover until OCR is used. Quicklio detects scan-like pages and, when you enable OCR in Editable Text mode, renders those pages locally and runs on-device English OCR to create editable Word text.
    OCR accuracy depends on scan quality, language, handwriting, and page layout, so review important text after conversion. Preserve Layout mode can keep the original scan exactly as it appears even when you do not need OCR.
    
    
    Make PDF text editable in Word
    Editable Text builds a real DOCX with editable text rather than flattening every page. The converter now keeps the original PDF page size and retains more heading size, bold/italic emphasis, paragraph alignment, relative indentation, paragraph spacing, and page boundaries when the PDF exposes enough positioning information.
    • Editable text instead of page screenshots.
    • Paragraph grouping and likely headings.
    • Basic bold/italic signals.
    • Left, center, and right alignment where it can be inferred.
    • Relative indentation and page dimensions.
    Complex tables, overlapping objects, forms, uncommon fonts, and exact multi-column geometry can still need cleanup because the PDF does not contain Word's original document structure.
    
    
    Use page ranges for large PDFs
    You do not have to convert the whole file. Enter a page range such as 1-8 or 2,5,10-14 to create a smaller Word document from only the pages you need. Preserve Layout automatically reduces render resolution for very long selections to control browser memory and DOCX size.
    If file size is the real problem instead, try Compress PDF.
    
    
    PDF to Word questions
    Is this PDF to Word converter free?
    Yes. You can convert supported PDFs to DOCX without creating an account, entering an email address, or paying Quicklio for the conversion.
    Does PDF to Word keep images and diagrams?
    Yes. Choose Preserve Layout + Images when images, diagrams, charts, vector graphics, backgrounds, columns, and page appearance need to stay together. Quicklio places a high-quality rendering of each selected PDF page into the Word file.
    Can I convert a scanned PDF to editable Word?
    Yes, for many scans. Choose Editable Text and enable OCR scanned pages before analysis. Quicklio runs English OCR in the browser and adds the recovered text to the DOCX.
    Can I edit the Word file after conversion?
    Editable Text mode creates real editable text. Preserve Layout mode gives a closer visual match, but its page snapshots are not individually editable because that is how the original formatting, images, and diagrams are kept together.
    Does Quicklio upload my PDF?
    No conversion upload is required. Parsing, scan detection, OCR when enabled, page rendering, and DOCX creation happen in your browser.
