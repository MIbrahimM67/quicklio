Typical desktop printing: duplex, landscape, flip on short edge, actual size. Printer drivers vary, so test a short signature first.

What is a signature?

A signature is a group of printed sheets that are folded together. This tool pads each signature to a multiple of four and arranges the page order automatically.

Why page order looks strange

Booklet imposition deliberately places high and low page numbers beside each other so they become sequential after printing and folding.

Test your printer

Duplex flip settings vary by printer. Print a small signature first before producing a full book.
