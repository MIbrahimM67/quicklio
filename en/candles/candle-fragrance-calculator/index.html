Why the percentage basis matters

If fragrance is 10% of wax weight, fragrance = wax × 0.10. If it is 10% of the finished mixture, fragrance = finished blend × 0.10.

Batch planning

Use the per-candle amount and quantity to plan a whole pour without manually multiplying each ingredient.

Calculation, not formulation advice
