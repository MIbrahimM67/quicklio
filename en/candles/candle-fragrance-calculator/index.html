Why the percentage basis matters

If fragrance is 10% of wax weight, fragrance = wax × 0.10. If it is 10% of the finished mixture, fragrance = finished blend × 0.10. Quicklio makes you choose instead of silently assuming.

Calculation, not formulation advice

Use the fragrance percentage specified for your actual wax, fragrance, recipe, and supplier guidance.
