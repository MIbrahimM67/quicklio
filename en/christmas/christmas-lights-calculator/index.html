How the Christmas tree estimate works

The tree calculation treats the tree as a cone. It estimates the length of a spiral at your chosen vertical spacing, then adds your buffer and rounds up to whole light strings.

Rooflines, fences, railings and garlands

Measure the actual path you want to decorate. Use a coverage multiplier of 1.0 for a straight run. If you plan to swag or wrap the lights, increase the multiplier to reflect the longer path.

Use the specifications on your actual lights

Enter the lighted length and number of bulbs for the string you plan to buy. Packaging, spacing, lead-wire length, connector limits, and electrical ratings vary by product, so the calculator does not invent those values.
