Frequently asked questions

What is an island in a stencil?

An island is an enclosed piece of material surrounded by a cut area, such as the center of an O. Without a bridge, that piece can fall out when the stencil is cut.

Can I turn a photo into a Cricut stencil?

Yes. Simplify the photo, remove tiny pieces, add bridges, then export SVG. Always inspect the SVG in your cutting software before sending it to the machine.

Does Quicklio upload my image?

No. The stencil processing runs in your browser.
