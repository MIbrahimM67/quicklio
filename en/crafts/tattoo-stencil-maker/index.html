Photo to tattoo stencil linework

The tool uses edge detection rather than simple black-and-white posterization, making it better suited to outlines, facial features, lettering references, and drawn artwork.

Control line weight before printing

Sensitivity changes how much detail survives. Line thickness can make faint edges easier to see on a printed or transfer-ready reference.

Mirror when your transfer workflow needs it

Some transfer processes reverse the image. The mirror option lets you prepare the orientation before printing.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a replacement for professional tattoo design?

No. It is a technical linework preparation tool. A qualified tattoo artist should review composition, anatomy, line weight, skin placement, and transfer quality.

Can I make a tattoo stencil from a photo?

Yes. High-contrast, uncluttered photos usually produce cleaner linework than busy backgrounds.

Can I print at a specific size?

Yes. Set the physical width before using Print / Save PDF and print at 100% scale.
