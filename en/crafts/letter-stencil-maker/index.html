Why bridges matter in letter stencils

Letters such as A, B, D, O, P, Q and numbers such as 0, 6, 8 and 9 contain enclosed centers. The bridge option adds narrow material connections so those centers are less likely to fall out of a cut stencil.

Large letter stencils

For large signs, increase the letter size, export SVG, and scale it in your print or cutting software. Keep the exported aspect ratio locked to avoid distorting the lettering.
