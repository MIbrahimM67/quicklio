Frequently asked questions

Can I make number stencils too?

Yes. The input accepts letters, numbers, spaces, and common punctuation.

Why do stencil letters need bridges?

Without a bridge, enclosed centers can become loose pieces after cutting. The bridge keeps them connected to the stencil sheet.

Can I export SVG?

Yes. Quicklio traces the generated stencil mask into SVG paths for use in compatible design and cutting software.
