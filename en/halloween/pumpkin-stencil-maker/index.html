How to get a cleaner pumpkin stencil

Start with a photo that has clear subject separation and decent lighting. Raise contrast to strengthen edges, adjust threshold to simplify light and dark areas, and add a little smoothing to remove tiny details that are difficult to carve.

2-tone vs 3-tone stencils

2-tone gives a classic black-and-white carving pattern. 3-tone keeps a middle gray region that can act as a guide for shallower carving or more nuanced shapes.
