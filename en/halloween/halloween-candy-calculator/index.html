How the Halloween candy calculation works

Quicklio multiplies expected trick-or-treaters by your pieces-per-child plan, adds your chosen buffer, then rounds up to whole bags. Because bags rarely divide perfectly into the target amount, the result also shows the expected leftover.

How much buffer should I add?

The buffer is your planning choice, not a universal recommendation. If your street varies from year to year, use a larger cushion. If you have reliable turnout history, you may need less.

Why save last year's count?

Your own address history is usually more useful than a generic neighborhood label. The saved number stays in this browser's local storage and is not sent to Quicklio.
