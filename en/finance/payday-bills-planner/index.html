What safe-to-spend means

This version subtracts bills due before your next payday from the balance you have now. It is a planning aid, not financial advice.

Recurring bills

Enter each bill by its normal day of the month. For shorter months, a day such as 31 is automatically moved to that month's last day.

Private by default

The planner does not connect to your bank. If you choose Save, the plan is stored only in this browser's local storage.
